What We Offer

Every detail, considered.

We offer end-to-end wedding planning with the kind of careful attention that turns a celebration into a memory your guests will carry for a lifetime.

Full-Service Planning

From the first vision call to the final farewell dance — we handle every element, vendor, and timeline so you can simply be present.

Décor & Florals

Catering & Menu

How It Works

Your planning journey.

A clear, calm process designed to make planning feel like a pleasure — not a project.

01

Discovery Call

We learn your story, your style, and your non-negotiables in a relaxed conversation — no forms, no pressure.

02

Vision & Budget

We translate your ideas into a clear concept, a realistic budget, and a shortlist of vendors who match your aesthetic.

03

Vendor Curation

We negotiate, book, and manage every vendor — from the venue to the dessert cart — on your behalf.

04

Day-of Execution

Our team is on-site from dawn until the last guest leaves, so you experience the day without running it.
